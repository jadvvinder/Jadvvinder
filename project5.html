Booking.com | Design Systems

Scaling Design for 200+ Global Teams

I joined the Design System team mid-stream to solve an adoption crisis. By building a proactive support infrastructure and tackling high-complexity components, we unified 40+ localized experiences and slashed dev cycles by 65%.

The Scaling Mess (Honest Context)

Booking.com was moving fast, but design was lagging. Teams were working in silos, creating 37+ variations of a single button. My challenge wasn't just fixing the library; it was fixing the trust between the System team and the Product teams.

The Efficiency Drain

Engineering teams wasted 30% of their sprints rebuilding UI components from scratch.

The Adoption Wall

Teams felt the central system was a "bottleneck" that didn't understand their local market needs.

Strategic Execution

1

DesignOps: A Support Ecosystem

I established "Office Hours" and a real-time support tier. We stopped being the "Design Police" and became consultants. This reduced frustration and turned 200+ teams into advocates.

2

Architecting "The Beast" (Adaptive Date Picker)

I designed the most complex component in the library. It had to handle ranges, pricing overlays, and 40+ languages. By modularizing this, we cut implementation time from 3 weeks to 2 hours.

3

Collaborative Brainstorming Platform

I created a platform where teams could pitch new patterns. If a pattern worked in an A/B test, we promoted it to the core library. This turned the Design System into a living organism.

SWOT Analysis: The System in Reality

Strengths

High adoption (94%) and extreme speed in launching experiments.

Opportunities

Automating design tokens directly into the CI/CD pipeline for engineers.

Weaknesses

Documentation was initially hard to search, requiring constant human support.

Threats

Rapid tech shifts in mobile required frequent component refactors.

The ROI

65%

Reduction in design-to-dev cycle time

94%

Visual consistency across 40+ markets

89%

Component reuse rate across new launches

My Role System Evolution & Advocacy Lead
Scope 200+ Independent Product Teams
Tools Figma (Tokens), Storybook, TypeScript

Key Senior Insight

"A design system is only as good as the support channel. If a designer gets stuck for more than 30 minutes, they'll build a custom component. My job was to ensure they never got stuck."
