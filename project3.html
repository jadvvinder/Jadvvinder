Leading a team of 6 designers to drive 30% revenue growth through user-centered strategy, accessible standards, and improved cross-functional delivery.

Lead Overview

The Mission

Standardize UX processes across a diverse EdTech portfolio while hitting aggressive revenue targets.

Team Leadership

Managed and mentored 6 designers, fostering a culture of high craft and inclusive design.

Governance

Impact

30% revenue increase and 25% faster time-to-market through operational optimization.

The Shift: From Ad-Hoc to Strategic

Before (Fragmented Effort)

  • • Siloed design efforts across different educational products.
  • • Inconsistent UI patterns leading to high maintenance costs.
  • • No formal accessibility standards for inclusive learning.

After (Unified Vision)

  • • Centralized design strategy focused on core conversion metrics.
  • • Shared component library reducing dev-handoff friction by 25%.
  • • Institutionalized WCAG compliance across the 1M+ user base.

DesignOps & Mentorship

As UX Lead, my focus was on building a sustainable environment where a team of 6 could thrive. I introduced three key operational pillars:

Quality Frameworks

Implemented weekly design critiques and craft reviews to maintain a high bar for visual and functional quality.

Dev-Design Sync

Established new handoff protocols and documentation standards that cut time-to-market by 25%.

Inclusive Culture

Mentored the team on inclusive design practices, ensuring products worked for children of all abilities.

Strategic Assessment (SWOT)

Strengths

Massive scale (1M+ users) and proven ability to tie UX improvements to revenue (30% growth).

Opportunities

Leveraging data-driven personalization to further improve student retention and engagement.

Weaknesses

Scaling a team of 6 required significant initial time investment in process definition over execution.

Threats

Increasing competition in the EdTech space requiring constant visual and functional innovation.
