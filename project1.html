Booking.com Enterprise SaaS

Optimizing Innovation: The A/B Testing Platform

Re-architecting a mission-critical internal tool to cut experiment setup time by 40%, enabling hundreds of product teams to ship data-validated features daily.

Executive Summary

The Mission

Redesign the internal "Experimentation Hub" to make complex data science accessible to product teams.

Target Audience

Designers, Product Managers, and Engineers across Booking.com.

Key Result

40% reduction in setup time; shift from 2-week lead times to self-service launches in days.

Governance

Implemented automated guardrails to prevent statistical errors in experiment design.

Behavioral Shift: Autonomy over Friction

Before (The Bottleneck)

  • •Teams relied heavily on data scientists for basic setup.
  • •UI was built for technical experts, excluding 60% of PMs.
  • •Manual validation led to 15% invalid experiment setups.

After (The Empowerment)

  • •Self-service wizards guided PMs through statistical setup.
  • •Real-time data visualization enabled fast go/no-go decisions.
  • •Experiment integrity improved by 25% via automated validation.

DesignOps: Democratizing Data

For an internal platform, documentation isn't enough. We built a support ecosystem to ensure every team felt confident running high-stakes tests.

In-App Education

Contextual tooltips explaining complex statistical terms (P-values, confidence intervals) in layman's terms.

Setup Wizards

Modular workflows that broke down experiment creation into 4 logical steps, eliminating cognitive overload.

Community Hub

Central platform to share Wins & Learnings across 15+ engineering clusters.

Strategic Impact

  • ✓ Simplified complex data science UI
  • ✓ Cut setup time by 40%
  • ✓ Cross-functional stakeholder buy-in
  • ✓ 25% test validity increase
"When designing for data experts, clarity beats beauty. When designing for the rest of the company, simplicity beats everything."
